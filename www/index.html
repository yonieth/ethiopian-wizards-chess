📜 History of Ethiopia & Royal Chess Pieces

🏛️ The Aksumite Empire & Senterej (ሰንጠረዥ)

For over a millennium, Ethiopian Emperors, Empresses, and Ras commanders played Senterej (ሰንጠረዥ)—Ethiopia's ancient royal strategy game passed down from the Kingdom of Aksum through the rock-hewn courts of Lalibela and the imperial castles of Gondar. In Ethiopian court tradition, chess was taught to nobility as an art of diplomacy, cavalry formation, and imperial defense.

⚔️ Ivory Marble vs. Volcanic Obsidian

Nigus (ንጉስ) reimagines this imperial heritage in 3D Wizard's Chess. The Ivory Marble & Brushed Gold Dynasty honors the carved limestone palaces and golden crowns of Aksum, while the Volcanic Obsidian & Ember Empire reflects the basalt highlands and Rift Valley lava shields of Ethiopia.
